# Email Writing Process

## Step 1 — Receive the category
User says which category to write from. Read `category.md` to get the blog URLs for that section.

## Step 2 — Read all blogs in that category
Fetch and read every blog in that category in parallel.

## Step 3 — Check angles-log
Read `angles-log.md` and filter out any angles already used.

## Step 4 — Suggest ideas
From the remaining angles, pick the ones with the strongest day-to-day on-call engineer angle (per the concept selection rule in `rules.md`) and present them. Wait for the user to pick one.

## Step 5 — Read rules.md
Read it fully before writing a single word.

## Step 6 — Write the email body
Follow the structure in `template.md`. Run every check in the self-check section of `rules.md` before sharing.

## Step 7 — Share body with user
Wait for user to approve, request changes, or iterate.

## Step 8 — Subject + preview + CTA
Once body is locked, present 5 subject-preview pairs and 5 CTA options together. Wait for user to pick one of each.

## Step 9 — Full email
Present the complete email with UTM links.

## Step 10 — Update angles-log
Log the angle, blog URL, subject used, and date in `angles-log.md`.
