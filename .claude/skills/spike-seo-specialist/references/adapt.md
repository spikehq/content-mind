# Adapt — good mechanics, wrong original context

4 tips whose original framing is e-commerce/affiliate/ad-driven, but the underlying mechanic is legitimately useful for Spike.sh once reframed.

### #2 — Boost Your Sales Through Reddit Screenshots
- Category: Content Optimization
- Original tip uses screenshots of Reddit comments recommending an affiliate product as social proof near a CTA. **Adapt:** use genuine customer testimonial screenshots (real Twitter/X posts, Reddit mentions of Spike, G2 reviews) placed near CTAs on landing pages and comparison pages — legitimate trust-building social proof, not affiliate-sales manipulation.

### #5 — Maximize Session Duration Using A Simple Layout
- Category: Technical SEO
- Add a "content hub"-style sidebar of related-article links alongside main content on blog.spike.sh and docs.spike.sh, to increase session duration and strengthen internal linking between related incident-management/on-call topics.

### #46 — Increase Revenue By Understanding Users' Behavior
- Category: Technical SEO
- Install Microsoft Clarity on key landing and blog pages, let it collect ~30 days of data, identify top-traffic URLs via GA, and review Clarity's Heatmaps to see exactly where users click/scroll/spend time most on those pages. **Adapt:** instead of placing ads/affiliate products in those high-engagement zones (the original tip), place primary CTAs there — "Start Free Trial," "Book a Demo," links to comparison pages — since that's proven to be where attention concentrates.

### #111 — Boost Sales Instantly From Discountable Products
- Category: SEO
- Original tip uses a sitewide notification-bar plugin to promote a product discount. **Adapt:** use the same sitewide notification bar for legitimate B2B promotions instead of discounts — e.g. announcing a free-trial extension, a major changelog release, a limited-seat webinar, or a private beta invite — since the mechanic (a persistent, hard-to-miss top bar) works for any time-limited, action-driving message, not just discounts.
